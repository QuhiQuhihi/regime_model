# From a completed replay to the next useful experiment

The current study implements rapid recognition, five/twenty-session revised labels,
historical interpretation and one explicit risk-recalibration policy across eight
assets. It yields a stable research map but negligible, uncertain calibration gains from the
feature-HMM resets, and a
poor result for directly forecasting risk from the fitted states. The conservative
observable threshold is almost silent; its low alert count is not successful detection.

Next, archive new daily research snapshots and the action taken after each alert.
Choose the acceptable review burden before changing thresholds. Test a dedicated correlation
change detector alongside the feature HMM, whose dependence feature has not separately
been validated as an alarm for pure correlation shifts. Test state-weighted calibration against the hard
20-session reset, with the same eligible dates and continuous fast baseline.

For an alpha-model application, specify which feature, training weight or regularization
parameter would change. Evaluate that action on subsequent residuals and decisions;
a better state map alone cannot establish the alpha model improved. For a hedging
application, include costs, executable timing, carry and realized hedge P&L.

These follow-on models and a prospective live review process have **not** been run.
The completed historical results and all alternative thresholds remain visible.
