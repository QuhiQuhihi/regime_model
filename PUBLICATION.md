# Research publication boundary

The practical study contains an executed notebook, research chapters, aggregate results
and original figures. Raw vendor histories and detailed daily paths remain ignored.
The source register and manifests identify inputs and their limitations; no unrestricted
redistribution or commercial-use permission for the data is asserted.

Before publication, run the commands in [reproduction](docs/05-reproduction.md).
`research/check_artifacts.py --public` checks links, executed outputs and blocked tracked
paths; the complete command additionally reconciles saved numerical results and hashes.
CI performs the offline checks and does not download prices or certify a full data rerun.

Retain source attribution and the distinction between historical replay, delayed labels,
synthetic diagnostics and unrun follow-up. Calibration losses are not investment returns.
The final date is the last complete common session, not an assertion of streaming coverage.

The earlier `old` branch and commits contain legacy material with unresolved source-data
rights; current-file cleanup does not clear Git history. Do not publish ignored recovery
folders or infer that a preserved older branch has received a new rights review.
Local assistant instructions and session state are ignored and must not be staged.
